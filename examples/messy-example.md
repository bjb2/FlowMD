# Weeknight Chili (Experiment)


~
Prep Time: 20ish minutes
Cook Time: 1 hour or so
Servings: feeds 3–4 people
Notes: Better the next day
~


1. Brown the meat. (Don’t rush this.)
2. Add onions, garlic, whatever peppers you have.
3. Dump in tomatoes and beans.
4. Simmer until it tastes right.

## Ingredients


- ground beef: ~1 lb
- onion: 1 large

- canned tomatoes: 2 cans
- beans (any kind)
- chili powder
- cumin
