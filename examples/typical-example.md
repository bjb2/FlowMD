# Pasta Aglio e Olio


~
Prep Time: 10 min
Cook Time: 15 min
Servings: 2
Cost: $5
~


1. Boil pasta in generously salted water until al dente.
2. Heat olive oil in a pan and add sliced garlic.
3. Cook garlic until lightly golden, then add red pepper flakes.
4. Toss pasta with oil mixture and finish with parsley.


? [ingredient] No parsley -> use basil or skip
? [step] Want protein -> add shrimp in step 2


## Ingredients


- pasta: 8 oz (spaghetti or linguine)
- olive oil: 1/3 cup
- garlic: 4 cloves, thinly sliced
- red pepper flakes
- parsley
